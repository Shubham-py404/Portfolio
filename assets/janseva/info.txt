I’m excited to share JanSeva AI, a project my teammate Shreshtha Gupta and I built to completely rethink how citizens interact with public services.

The Problem: 
Navigating government portals whether applying for a passport, registering a vehicle, or paying a traffic challan is often fragmented and time-consuming. Finding the correct portal and hunting for specific options across cluttered UIs is a frustrating experience. We wanted to eliminate this bureaucratic friction.

The Solution: 
We built JanSeva AI as a centralized, conversational digital assistant. Instead of forcing users to navigate complex menus across multiple websites, we brought the services to them.

Key Features: 
->  Conversational UI: Replaces endless web pages with dynamic, interactive  
      application  forms generated right inside the chat window. 
->  Smart Context: Remembers your conversation history (like Challan IDs, 
      application  references, or personal details) so you never have to type the  
      same information twice.
->  Instant Guidance: Cuts through complex jargon to provide accurate, 
      easy-to-  understand procedural rules. 
->  The "Smart Wrapper" Approach: The AI acts as an intelligent layer over  
      existing government infrastructure. Users simply ask to apply or check a 
      status, and the AI securely fetches or inserts data into the respective 
      department's backend on their behalf   (demonstrated via two simulated   
      department portals). This means we don't have to rebuild legacy systems,    
      the AI just wraps around them seamlessly.

While we didn’t make the cut this time around, we know it’s a prototype and it definitely has its flaws and edge cases to iron out. However, we set out to solve a very real, everyday problem—and we strongly believe this concept has immense potential to make government services truly accessible for everyone.
